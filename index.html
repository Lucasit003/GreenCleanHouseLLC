What we offer

The right clean for every home

Whether you need a quick refresh or a full reset, we'll match the clean to what your home actually needs — using plant-based products safe for kids and pets.

Weekly / Biweekly

Regular Clean

The backbone of a consistently fresh home. We handle the essentials so you can spend your time on what matters most.

  • Kitchen surfaces, sinks & appliances
  • Bathrooms — toilets, tubs & mirrors
  • Vacuuming & mopping all floors
  • Dusting & tidying throughout
  • Trash emptied & liners replaced
Most thorough

Deep Clean

For when you want a true reset. We go into every corner, behind every appliance, and under every surface.

  • Everything in a Regular Clean
  • Inside oven, fridge & microwave
  • Baseboards, vents & ceiling fans
  • Inside cabinets & drawers
  • Interior windows, sills & frames
  • Grout scrubbing in baths & kitchen
One-time

Move-In / Move-Out

Leave your old place spotless or welcome yourself into a fresh start. Perfect for tenants, landlords, and real estate turnovers.

  • Full deep clean of all rooms
  • Inside all appliances & cabinets
  • Closets & storage areas
  • Bathroom detail clean
  • Final walk-through included
