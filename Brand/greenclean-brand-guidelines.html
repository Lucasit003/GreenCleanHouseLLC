The idea

One mark holds the whole name

Green Clean House cleans homes the greener way. The logo doesn't decorate that promise — it spells it out. Every part of the symbol maps to one word of the name, so the mark works even when the words aren't there.

G

Green

The eucalyptus body of the house — calm, botanical, a nod to plant-based products and a healthier home.

C

Clean

The two sunbeam sparkles — the visible glint of a room that's just been finished.

H

House

The rounded silhouette with an open, welcoming door — every house treated like our own.

Logo

The primary lockup

The vertical lockup is the default — use it wherever there's room to breathe. The mark sits above the wordmark, set in Fraunces, with the descriptor underneath.

Horizontal lockup — for headers, signage, and wide spaces.

The mark alone — app icons, stamps, favicons, social.

Reversed on Deep Pine — the standard dark application.

White on Eucalyptus.

One-colour Pine — faxes, etching, single-ink print.

One-colour white — for photography and dark panels.

Spacing & scale

Give it room, keep it legible

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the height of the doorway on all sides. Nothing — type, edges, other logos — enters this zone.

Minimum size

The mark stays crisp down to 24 px on screen and 9 mm in print. Below that, the doorway and sparkles start to fill in.

Misuse

Six things never to do

Consistency is what makes a small business look established. Don't redraw, recolour, or crowd the logo.

× Don't stretch or squash
× Don't rotate or tilt
× Don't recolour the mark
× Don't add shadows or effects
× Don't place on busy backgrounds
× Don't add or move elements

Voice & tone

Warm, plain-spoken, reassuring

Talk like a trusted neighbour, not a corporation. Cleaning a home is personal — the voice should feel safe, specific, and free of hype.

We sound like

  • Friendly and down-to-earth
  • Specific about what's included
  • Calm and reassuring about trust & access
  • Proud of greener, low-tox products

We avoid

  • Corporate jargon and buzzwords
  • Over-promising or fake urgency
  • Shaming people about mess
  • All-caps shouting and exclamation pile-ups
